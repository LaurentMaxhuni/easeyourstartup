# EaseYourStartup launch promo — proposal

The user approved the seven-beat storyboard and selected sketches first. This brief records the approved website-embed, 16:9, 33-second English promo and BAB arc. Voice and audio provider remain unselected.

## Intent

Make a cinematic 33-second web promo for solo founders and lean product teams announcing a product or update without dedicated marketing support. The story moves from the founder's blank-page moment to an evidence-led, channel-ready launch kit. Warm, practical, quietly confident; use the product's existing off-white, graphite, and cobalt palette.
